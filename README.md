# Software Engineering Research Lab

A research and experimentation portfolio exploring Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering.

## Overview

This repository documents my academic exploration and practical experiments in Software Engineering, with a particular interest in the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and software development.

The goal is to study existing research, investigate relevant problems, implement experimental approaches, and evaluate their results through reproducible experiments.

## Research Directions

My initial areas of exploration include:

- **AI for Software Engineering:** Exploring how AI can support software development activities.
- **Software Testing and Quality:** Investigating approaches to software testing, defect detection, and quality assessment.
- **Large Language Models for Software Engineering:** Studying the capabilities and limitations of LLMs in software engineering tasks.

These directions are exploratory and may evolve as the literature review progresses.

## Research Approach

The work in this repository follows an iterative research process:

1. Literature review and problem identification.
2. Formulation of research questions.
3. Experimental design and implementation.
4. Evaluation and analysis of results.
5. Documentation and reproducibility.
